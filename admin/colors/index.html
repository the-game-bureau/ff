Mugshot Colour Lab

Checking credentials...

A suspect's two colours are normally sampled from their mugshot, and the sampler takes whatever covers the most pixels — which in a dark room or against a grey wall is the background, not the person. This is where those get fixed by hand.

Click a Primary or Secondary chip to arm it, then click anywhere on a mugshot to take that pixel. Hover first: the loupe magnifies what is under the cursor and the readout names the hex. The chip stays armed, so keep clicking until it looks right. Any mugshot works, not only that suspect's, so a colour can be lifted off someone else's photo. Edits live in this browser until you paste them into the source.
