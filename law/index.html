Law

  • Each week, you pick one NFL team as your victim, the one you're certain will lose. You can name any team whose game is more than 5 minutes from kicking off.
  • If they lose, you slide by for the week.
  • If they win or tie, that's your case closed. DUN DUN.
  • Each NFL team can be chosen only once per season.
  • Fill your weeks in order. If an earlier week has no victim named, the board stays shut until it does.
  • You can change your choice up to 5 minutes before your current victim's game kicks off. You can only change it to a team that has not kicked off. Once your victim locks, that week is settled and nothing else on the board can be taken, whatever time its own game starts.
  • You can name victims for the whole season right now and change them week by week if you'd like.
  • Name nobody and you are out too. Once the last game of the week has kicked off, a week with no victim named is a case closed the same as a wrong one.
  • Our game will continue into the playoffs if necessary. All playoff teams will be available in the first week of the playoffs.
  • If more than one team makes it through the Super Bowl or if the final teams “go out” in the same week then there will be co-winners.
