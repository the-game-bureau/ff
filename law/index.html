The Law

  • Each week, before your team kicks off, you pick one NFL team as your victim, the one you're certain will lose.
  • If they lose, you slide by for the week.
  • If they win or tie, that's your case closed. DUN DUN.
  • Each NFL team can be chosen only once per season.
  • Our game will continue into the playoffs if necessary. All playoff teams will be available in the first week of the playoffs.
  • If more than one team makes it through the Super Bowl or if the final teams “go out” in the same week then there will be co-winners.
